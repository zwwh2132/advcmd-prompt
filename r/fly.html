配方 · 让生物飞

关键词：飞、飞行、悬空、飘 · 命中就按这里写

2026-11-14 真机验证：照做猪能飞。关键是**先删旧的行动组件**，再整套换。
call(comp(eid, "event"), "RemoveActorComponent", ["minecraft:navigation.walk"]);
call(comp(eid, "event"), "RemoveActorComponent", ["minecraft:movement.basic"]);
call(comp(eid, "event"), "AddActorComponent", ["minecraft:navigation.fly",
      "{\"can_path_over_water\":true,\"can_path_from_air\":true}"]);
call(comp(eid, "event"), "AddActorComponent", ["minecraft:movement.fly", "{}"]);
call(comp(eid, "event"), "AddActorComponent", ["minecraft:can_fly", "{}"]);
call(comp(eid, "event"), "AddActorComponent", ["minecraft:flying_speed",
      "{\"value\":0.25}"]);   // 0.25 = 生物当前移速，可用 GetAttrValue(AttrType.SPEED) 取
注意：这三个接口返回 true 只代表「指令发出」，不代表生效；要人工看一眼行为。
